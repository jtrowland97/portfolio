About Me

I am a proud graduate of the University of Texas with a bachelor’s degree in English and current Master of Arts in Technical Communication student at Texas State University. I’ve been working with UT High School, Texas’s first online high school in operation since 1999, for over six years as a Senior Student Support Specialist, helping prospective parents and students enroll and be successful in online school.

I’m a supporter of public education, and I enjoy working in the public sector. I’ve been an Austin resident for ten years, and outside of work, you’ll find me at Alamo Drafthouse every weekend
