How to Process Independent Study Program Course Extensions

A step-by-step guide for UT High School staff who process course extension requests. It walks through three systems (MySchoolBucks, the Certification/Enrollment Tool in Canvas, and an extension tracking sheet), covers what to do when a student isn’t eligible, and includes ready-to-send email language for both outcomes.

What this sample shows

  • Numbered steps with bold labels for every button, menu, and field a reader needs to find
  • Annotated screenshots, with arrows and boxes that show where to click
  • Tip and Important callouts for the rules readers are most likely to miss, such as which expiration date to give the student
  • A decision branch (Step 8a) for students who aren’t eligible, with its own email template
Audience
UT High School support staff
